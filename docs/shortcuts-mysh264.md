# Every shortcut in mysh264's setup

Everything a key, a mouse button, a corner of the screen or the touchpad
does in [mysh264's setup](mysh264.md): the setup of hyprsimple-nix's
maintainer, with [the keys in i3's style](settings.md#keys-in-i3s-style). If you
use that setup, these are your keys.

* [Every shortcut](shortcuts.md) lists hyprsimple's own keys, as they come.
* To see the keys you have now, press **SUPER + /**.
* To change any of them, see [Keys, mouse buttons and the touchpad](keys.md).

Key names: **SUPER** is the Windows key. **← → ↑ ↓** are the arrow keys.
**ENTER** is the Return key. **Numpad 4** is the 4 on the number pad, the
block of numbers on the right of big keyboards. **-** and **=** are the two
keys after 0.

## Window groups and the i3 layout

On a normal workspace these work on groups: windows stacked as tabs. On a workspace in the i3 layout, they work on its tabs and splits. Workspaces 2 and 3 start in the i3 layout.

| Keys | What happens |
|---|---|
| **SUPER + G** | Makes the windows a group of tabs, or undoes it. i3 layout: tabs, or side by side |
| **SUPER + SHIFT + G** | Takes the window out of its group |
| **SUPER + SHIFT + ←** | Moves the window into the group on the left. i3 layout: moves the window left |
| **SUPER + SHIFT + →** | Moves the window into the group on the right. i3 layout: moves the window right |
| **SUPER + SHIFT + ↑** | Moves the window into the group above. i3 layout: moves the window up |
| **SUPER + SHIFT + ↓** | Moves the window into the group below. i3 layout: moves the window down |
| **SUPER + ALT + 1** | Window 1 of the group, or tab 1 |
| **SUPER + ALT + 2** | Window 2 of the group, or tab 2 |
| **SUPER + ALT + 3** | Window 3 of the group, or tab 3 |
| **SUPER + ALT + 4** | Window 4 of the group, or tab 4 |
| **SUPER + ALT + 5** | Window 5 of the group, or tab 5 |
| **SUPER + ALT + 6** | Window 6 of the group, or tab 6 |
| **SUPER + ALT + 7** | Window 7 of the group, or tab 7 |
| **SUPER + ALT + 8** | Window 8 of the group, or tab 8 |
| **SUPER + ALT + 9** | Window 9 of the group, or tab 9 |
| **SUPER + A** | Switches the workspace between Hyprland's layout and i3's |
| **SUPER + H** | i3 layout: the next window you open goes beside this one |
| **SUPER + J** | i3 layout: the next window you open goes below this one |

## Hot corners

Move the pointer into a corner of the screen. A corner works once; move away
from it, and it works again. Corners rest while a window is full screen.
[Hot corners](corners.md) shows how to give them other jobs.

| Keys | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Top left corner** | Hides the bar, or shows it again |
| **Top right corner** | Shows the desktop (an empty workspace); there again, back to where you were |
| **Bottom right corner** | Back to the workspace you were on before |
| **Bottom left corner** | Nothing |

## The touchpad

| Keys | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Tap with 1 finger** | Left click |
| **Tap with 2 fingers** | Right click |
| **Tap with 3 fingers** | Middle click: pastes the text you selected |
| **Tap, tap again and slide** | Drags; lift the finger to let go |
| **Slide 2 fingers up or down** | Scrolls. The page follows your fingers, like on a phone |
| **Slide 2 fingers left or right** | Scrolls sideways |
| **Press down at the bottom right** | Right click |
| **Press down anywhere else** | Left click |

While you type, the touchpad rests, so your palm cannot move the pointer.
